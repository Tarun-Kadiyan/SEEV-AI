# Recruitment campaigns
Create a campaign in either company screening page. Its JD and matching method are fixed. Select the same campaign for future PDF or CSV/XLSX uploads. Rankings are saved in SQLite and reload on login. Duplicate normalized text within a campaign is skipped; this is not identity verification. Different candidates with identical text may be merged; changed resumes are distinct. Earlier unassigned analyses remain in History; they are not automatically imported. Equal scores retain insertion order. MiniLM fallback is blocked for MiniLM campaigns to preserve comparability. Scores support human review only. Full campaign CSV includes all candidates regardless of display filter.
