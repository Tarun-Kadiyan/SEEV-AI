Writing review is a deterministic editorial checklist, not AI authorship detection. Percentage is the proportion of text segments (at least five words) containing listed generic phrases. A low value does not prove human authorship; a high value does not prove AI use. It is excluded from fit scores and ranking. New analyses store the review in existing result JSON. Older records show unavailable. Standalone individual writing checks do not create a screening history entry. Company rankings, dashboard exports and analysis detail show the editorial measure. No new dependencies.
